The long version

Five turns, one direction.

I started in materials science: chemical engineering in Lyon, battery research in Glasgow, aircraft composites at Airbus. At Saint-Gobain I developed switchable glass for cars, and noticed the parts of the job I looked forward to were the human ones. Explaining a failed test, writing the patent, training the new hires. So in 2022 I did a web development master's at night, to see whether I would like building software as much as explaining it. I did. Developer, consultant, tech lead, and now product manager.

The shape of it 2015 → now

  1. 2015–2018 · Where it started

    Materials science

    Chemical engineering in Lyon, then two materials master's. Research on Li-ion energy storage at the University of Glasgow, and on composite materials for electric aircraft at Airbus.

  2. 2019–2022 · Saint-Gobain

    R&D engineer

    Switchable glass for cars: supplier screening, product qualification, patents, and a lot of technical writing. Also the start of mentoring, which never stopped.

  3. 2022–2023 · Nights and weekends

    Learning to code

    A web development master's on top of the day job, to find out if I would enjoy building software. Then my first developer role: an internal app at Saint-Gobain that people used every day.

  4. 2023–2025 · Consulting

    Fullstack to tech lead

    Monolith to modular architecture for a European e-commerce group. Then a greenfield app for a sustainability client as tech lead, and a three-day feasibility sprint for healthcare e-commerce.

  5. Now · Product

    AI × Product

    Product manager at Netlight. First a Slack-to-Teams migration for several thousand people, now a tooling landscape run as a product domain. After hours: the Lab.

That's the story. The receipts are in the Lab.
